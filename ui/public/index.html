Wolfgang AI

Wolfgang AI - Your Personal GameMaster!

Wolfgang is a small open source AI Storyteller, that will be a Game Master for you if you desire. Start a new chat if desired or return to an old one.

Play in any world you choose by adding documents about it, so that Wolfgang can reference details in character sheets, documents and your chat history for fine-tuned sessions.

With the launch on peerlist we have shown, that Wolfgang works well for people. As you can see on our GitHub, we are quickly fixing bugs and constantly add more human-made training data to improve Wolfgang and make playing with him more fun!

Why build yet another chatbot?

We believe there to be a need for self-hostable solutions as well as a gap in actual game master AIs. Most so-called game masters AIs take over player characters on a regular basis or lose important information.

If you are interested in how we plan to solve the issue, head over to our docs on GitHub.

How is this financed?

Right now, in its early test phase, the whole project is paid for by Björn 'Idrinth' Büttner. Donations are highly encouraged to cover hosting costs and paid tiers will likely be added to the hosted version.
