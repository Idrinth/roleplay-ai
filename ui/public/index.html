Wolfgang AI

Your Personal GameMaster!

Play role-playing games from anywhere at any time!

Wolfgang is a small open source AI Storyteller, that will be a Game Master for you if you desire. Start a new Role-Play-Session!

Play in any world you choose by adding documents about it, so that Wolfgang can reference details in character sheets, documents and your chat history for fine-tuned sessions.

Statistics

    Launches

    With the launch on peerlist we have shown, that Wolfgang works well for 30+ people. As you can see on our GitHub, we are quickly fixing bugs and constantly add more human-made training data to improve Wolfgang and make playing with him more fun!

    Build By

    How It Works

    1. Create a new world by starting a chat
    2. Create your character and choose your world(wizard optional)
    3. Upload relevant documents (optional)
    4. Start playing - Wolfgang handles the rest!

    Our Features

    Near unlimited context Long role-play-sessions and detailed worlds possible
    Open-Source Always free to host yourself
    Fine-tuned LLMs Better output compared to generalists
    Document Integration Reference character sheets and world details for consistency
    Privacy-First Hosting You don't even need to provide your e-mail, additionally we host the data in the EU
    Cross-Platform The UI works in browsers everywhere, from small mobile devices to large screens on desktop

    Why build yet another chatbot?

    We believe there to be a need for self-hostable solutions as well as a gap in actual game master AIs. Most so-called game master AIs take over player characters on a regular basis or lose important information.

    If you are interested in how we plan to solve the issue, head over to our docs on GitHub.

    How is this financed?

    Right now, in its early test phase, the whole project is paid for by Björn 'Idrinth' Büttner. Donations are highly encouraged to cover hosting costs and paid tiers will likely be added to the hosted version.

    The locally hosted, private instance will ALWAYS be free.
